Creator AI Studio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Creator AI Studio ("the app") is published by Muhammad Naveed ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

1. Information we collect

  • Text you enter. The topics, keywords and options you type into the tools (captions, Reels scripts, hashtags, YouTube text, post and business copy) are sent to our server and to Google's Gemini API to generate the result. Please do not enter personal, financial or confidential information.
  • Anonymous device identifier. The app creates a random ID stored on your device. We use it to count daily usage (free generation limits and rewarded ads) and to link a Pro subscription to your device. It is not your name, phone number or email.
  • Profile details (optional). If you save a profile in the app, it is stored locally on your device.
  • Subscription information. If you subscribe to Pro, Amazon processes the payment. We receive a purchase receipt and Amazon's user identifier, which we verify with Amazon to activate Pro. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • Advertising data. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, with mediation partners such as Unity Ads. These services may collect your advertising ID, IP address and device information to serve and measure ads.

2. How we use information

  • To generate the content you request.
  • To enforce daily limits, grant rewarded-ad bonuses and activate Pro features.
  • To show ads in the free version and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To keep the service running and fix errors.

3. Service providers

We use these providers, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google (Gemini API, AdMob)
  • Unity Technologies (Unity Ads, through AdMob mediation)
  • Cloudflare (hosting our server and database)
  • Amazon Appstore (distribution and subscription billing)

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data retention and deletion

Usage counters and subscription records are kept only as long as needed to run the app. Text you submit is processed to produce a response and is not stored by us as part of your account. The app has no user accounts. To request deletion of data linked to your device ID, email us at the address below. Uninstalling the app removes the local data on your device.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
