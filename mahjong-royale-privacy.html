Mahjong Royale – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mahjong Royale handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

Mahjong Royale does not require an account or ask you to provide your name, email address, or phone number to play.

Game progress, coins, level information, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Mahjong Royale uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, and optional rewarded video advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process information such as IP address, advertising ID or other device identifiers, advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and app performance data. This information may be used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

Rewarded videos are optional. The game grants its advertised in-game reward after a rewarded video is successfully completed.

Google may share information with its advertising partners as described in its policies.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

3. Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required, the app may display a consent form before requesting advertisements. You can also review advertising and privacy choices through your device's available settings.

4. Data Storage and Deletion

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game may delete this locally stored progress.

Advertising-related information is handled by Google and its partners under their respective policies and retention practices. We do not control their data retention systems.

5. Permissions

The game uses internet connectivity for advertising services. It does not require access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise GPS location for its gameplay.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information directly to us. Advertising services may have their own age-related requirements and settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
