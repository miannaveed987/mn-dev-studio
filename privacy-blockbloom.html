Block Bloom - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Block Bloom ("the app") is a puzzle game published by MN Dev Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. Your best score and in-game coins are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by third-party advertising partners: Google AdMob, Unity Ads and ironSource (Unity LevelPlay). These partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and ad interaction data to show ads and measure their performance. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Rewarded ads

You may choose to watch a video ad to receive extra coins or to continue a game. Watching ads is always optional.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. The only data leaving your device is the ad-related data described above, which is handled by our advertising partners under their own privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
