NoxChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

NoxChat ("the app", "we", "us") is a chat and calling app published by Muhammad Naveed (MN Dev Studio). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, username, password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password) and, if you choose Google sign-in, your Google account name and email.
  • Profile photo: the photo you upload, if any.
  • Content you send: text messages, photos and voice messages you send to other users, and your messages with AI characters inside the app.
  • Calls: technical call information needed to connect and bill a call (who called whom, time, duration, call type). We do not record call audio or video.
  • Wallet and purchases: your coin balance, Premium status, rewarded-ad history and purchase receipts from the Amazon Appstore.
  • Device and notification data: a push notification token, online/offline status, and basic device and usage data collected by our service providers.
  • Advertising data: the app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID and similar identifiers to serve and measure ads.

2. How we use information

  • To create your account, let you chat and call other users, and show your profile to people you chat with.
  • To deliver messages, photos, voice messages and push notifications.
  • To run the coin wallet, Premium subscriptions and rewarded ads, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To show ads that support the free app.
  • To keep the service secure and to fix problems.

3. Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf under their own terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Messaging) – accounts, messages, presence, notifications.
  • Cloudflare (Workers and storage) – our server logic and storage of photos and voice messages.
  • Google AdMob – advertising.
  • Amazon Appstore – in-app purchases and payment processing. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • AI provider – messages you send to an AI character are sent to a third-party AI service to generate replies.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing

Messages and media are visible to the people you send them to. Your username, display name and profile photo are visible to other NoxChat users. We share information with the providers listed above, and when required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and the data associated with it by emailing mian.987.mn@gmail.com from the email address you registered with. We will respond within a reasonable time. Some records, such as purchase records, may be kept where the law requires.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with reputable providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

NoxChat is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Your choices

  • You can change your name and profile photo in the app at any time.
  • You can limit ad personalisation in your device's settings (Google → Ads).
  • You can turn off notifications in your device settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
