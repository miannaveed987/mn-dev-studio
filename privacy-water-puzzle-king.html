Privacy Policy

Water Puzzle King (the "App") is a free puzzle game published by MN Dev Studio. Last updated: November 24, 2026.

1. Information we collect

The App does not require an account and we do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information. We do not run our own servers for this App and we do not collect or store personal data about you.

Your game progress (current level, coins and sound setting) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. If you uninstall the App, this data is removed.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The App shows ads (banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads) to keep the game free. To do this, we use the following third-party advertising services:

  • Google AdMob: how Google uses data
  • Unity Ads: Unity Privacy Policy
  • ironSource / Unity LevelPlay: Unity Privacy Policy

These services may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and operating system information, and information about how you interact with ads. They use this information to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where allowed, personalize ads. We do not control this data collection. Please read the policies above to learn more.

3. Your choices

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (on Android: Settings, then Google, then Ads). You can also use the sound switch inside the App to turn game sounds off.

4. Children

The App is a general audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will help.

5. Data security and retention

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not store it. Game progress stays on your device.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.
