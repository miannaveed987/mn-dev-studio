NoxChat Terms & Community Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Zero tolerance for objectionable content

NoxChat has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. You must not post, send or share:

  • Hate speech, slurs, threats, harassment or bullying
  • Sexually explicit or pornographic content, or any content involving minors in a sexual or harmful way
  • Violence, self-harm encouragement, or illegal activity
  • Spam, scams, fraud or impersonation

2. Filtering

Messages are automatically checked for abusive and objectionable language. Messages that fail the check are not sent.

3. Report and block

Every message from another user has a Report button and a Block button. Blocking removes that user from your experience immediately. Reports are sent to our moderation team.

4. How we respond

We review reports within 24 hours. Objectionable content is removed, and users who break these rules are warned, suspended or permanently banned.

5. AI characters

Some characters in NoxChat are fictional AI characters, not real people. Their replies are generated automatically and are also subject to content rules.

6. Purchases

Premium and coin packs are bought through the app store you installed NoxChat from. Refunds follow that store's policy.

8. Acceptance

By using NoxChat you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.
