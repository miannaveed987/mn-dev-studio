Privacy Policy

Village Cricket Kings 3D · Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Village Cricket Kings 3D ("the app", "the game"), published by MN Dev Studio ("we", "us"), handles information when you play the game. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account to play offline matches, and we do not collect your name, phone number, address or precise location.

  • Game data stored on your device: settings, coins, player and match statistics, career progress and tournament progress are saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data.
  • Advertising data: the app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, app usage and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • Online features: if you use the online room or multiplayer features, a player name or identifier and the match actions needed to run the room may be processed through our online service provider.

2. How information is used

  • To run the game and save your progress on your device.
  • To show and measure advertising, which helps keep the game free.
  • To run online rooms and matches, where available.
  • To fix bugs and respond to support requests.

3. Third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob. These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob / Google Ads: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your choices

  • You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example, by resetting or deleting your advertising ID).
  • You can delete all locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

5. Children

Village Cricket Kings 3D is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the limited data that reaches us. Game data on your device stays on your device until you delete it. Data held by third-party services is kept according to their own policies.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.

8. Contact us
